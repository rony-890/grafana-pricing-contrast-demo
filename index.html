What the spot check found

On Grafana’s public pricing page, axe-core 4.14.0 and Lighthouse 13.6.0 each reported 21 color-contrast nodes at a 1280 × 900 desktop viewport. One small calculator note (.adaptive-note) was reported at 2.47:1, below 4.5:1 for normal text.
